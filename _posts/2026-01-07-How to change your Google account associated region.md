Google account has an associated region to provide services complied with the local laws and regulations. 

There is a way to change your account associated region with [Request to Change Associated Region page](https://policies.google.com/country-association-form?authuser).

You also can find this page with the follow steps: 

1. visiting Google Privacy & Terms site
2. select the FAQ section
3. Look for the question: "Why is my account associated with a region?" .
4. Under the section titled "Determining the country associated with your account," click the link to the "change your location" request page.

And please keep in mind that your request may be declined since Google have some mechanisms to verify the accuracy of the information provided.
